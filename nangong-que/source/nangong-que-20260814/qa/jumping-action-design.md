# Jumping Row Character Action

The fixed Codex v2 row id remains `jumping`, but 南宫阙 does not perform a physical jump.

## Approved motion concept

Use a grounded, dignified spell-seal loop that fits her mature cultivator identity:

1. Front-facing composed stance with hands gathered.
2. Right hand rises into a refined two-finger spell seal; left hand steadies near the waist.
3. Both wide sleeves open in a controlled arc; the seal is held before the chest and the gaze becomes focused and authoritative.
4. Sleeves draw inward while the seal is released.
5. Return smoothly to the original gathered-hands stance.

## Motion constraints

- Both feet remain planted on one baseline in every frame.
- No jump, hop, takeoff crouch, airborne pose, floating, levitation, landing, or vertical displacement.
- Preserve canonical height, head size, scale, face, costume, and identity.
- Motion is carried by fingers, forearms, sleeves, and restrained gaze changes.
- No magic circle, aura, glow, particles, symbols, weapons, detached effects, shadows, dust, motion marks, or new props.
- Keep hands anatomically clear, sleeves attached, and every pose fully inside its slot.
